# 🚀 starfield-dodge

Dodge as many asteroids as you can in this fast-paced space survival game! ☄️

## 🎮 Gameplay

Pilot your ship through an endless starfield while homing asteroids close in from every direction. The longer you survive, the faster and more aggressive they become — good luck! 💀

- ⏱️ **Score** is based on how long you survive
- ☄️ **Asteroids home in** on your position and get relentless over time
- 💥 **Asteroids collide** with each other and explode, but don't count on it saving you
- 🏆 **Best score** is tracked per session

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Keys |
|--------|------|
| Move left / right | `←` `→` or `A` `D` |
| Move up / down | `↑` `↓` or `W` `S` |

## ✨ Features

- 🌌 Multi-layer parallax starfield with twinkling stars
- 🔥 Engine trail and explosion particle effects
- 📳 Screen shake on impact
- 🔴 Danger vignette when asteroids get close
- 🛸 Ship tilts dynamically based on movement direction
